About

I am currently a tenure-track Associate Professor at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Tongji University, Shanghai. Before joining Tongji, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Research Institute for Science and Engineering, Waseda University, working under Prof. Shibata Yoshihiro.

I obtained my PhD in mathematics in September 2017 from Université Paris-Est Créteil Val-de-Marne, under the supervision of Prof. Danchin Raphaël.

Office Ningjing Building 203, Siping Road 1239, Shanghai 200092, China Email xinzhang2020@tongji.edu.cn

Research

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations arising in fluid dynamics, with particular emphasis on free boundary value problems and multi-phase flows.

Navier-Stokes equations Free surface problems Two-phase flows Compressible fluids Maximal regularity Decay estimates
